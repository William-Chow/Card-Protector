Privacy Policy

Card Number Protector (shown on your phone as “Card Pro”) · Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version

  • The card numbers and other text you mask are processed on your phone and never leave it.
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. Those two Google services collect limited device information, described below.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Card Number Protector is an Android app that masks card numbers and other sensitive details in text, so you can share it safely. In this policy, “we” means the developer of the app. You can reach us at work.dash10101@gmail.com.

The text you mask stays on your phone

Text you type into the app, paste into it, share to it from another app, or select and send to it with the “Redact” option is processed entirely on your device. It is never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.

  • The app does not save that text to your phone’s storage, and it is excluded from Android backups.
  • It is never included in crash reports.
  • The masked result goes only where you send it: when you copy it, replace the selected text with it, or share it.
  • If you share a card image, the app writes the masked image to its own private storage so that the app you share it with can read it. The image contains only the masked number.

Settings stored on your phone

The app remembers four display choices: the theme, the masking symbol, and how many digits of a card number to reveal and from which end. These are stored on your device. They can be included in your phone’s Android backup or device-to-device transfer. Nothing else the app stores is included.

Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows banner and full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, information about your device, and how you interact with ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy options” in the app’s menu.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone’s settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

  • the technical details of the crash (the stack trace) and the state of the app when it happened;
  • information about your device at that moment, such as the model and Android version;
  • a random installation ID created by Crashlytics, and the Firebase installation ID, which let us count how many installations a crash affects.

Crash reports do not contain any text you entered and are not used for advertising. Firebase keeps them for 90 days and then deletes them. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

App updates: Google Play

When the app starts, it asks the Google Play Store whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update. This check is handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads, crash reports and the update check. Masking works without a connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, as described above.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which act on our behalf. We do not receive the text you mask at all.

Security

Data sent to Google AdMob, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Play is encrypted in transit.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or change your ad consent as described above.
  • Because the app has no accounts, crash reports cannot be linked to you by name. If you have a question or a request about your data, email us and we will help where we can.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
